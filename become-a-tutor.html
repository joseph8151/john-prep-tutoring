AN IMPORTANT NOTE

Native English Comes
in Many Backgrounds.

존프랩 welcomes tutors who speak English naturally at a native or fully native level. This can include:

  • People raised in the United States or Canada
  • People educated primarily in English in North America
  • Bilingual Korean-Americans or Korean-Canadians
  • Second-generation Korean diaspora
  • Heritage Korean speakers whose dominant or native language is English
  • Other candidates with demonstrably native-level North American English

Strong English ability alone is not the only consideration — we also value natural pronunciation, communication style, teaching ability, experience with children, professionalism, and personality fit.

Our standard is language proficiency and teaching suitability — not ethnicity, race or appearance. We do not use appearance as a hiring criterion, and we do not exclude qualified candidates based on ethnic background.

Korean-American? Korean-Canadian?
Bilingual and Native in English?
We'd Love to Hear From You.

If you grew up in an English-speaking environment and English is your native or dominant language, you are welcome to apply. Many families appreciate tutors who can provide authentic North American English while also understanding Korean family culture.

Korean fluency is not required unless a specific role calls for it.
